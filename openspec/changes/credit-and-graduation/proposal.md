## Why

Progress tracks course completion but cannot express credits, study-hour requirements, program completion, or graduation eligibility.

## What Changes

- Add credit values and auditable credit awards.
- Add graduation programs with required courses, credit categories, minimum totals, and expiry rules.
- Provide learner degree-audit and administrator award/revocation workflows.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `credit-and-graduation`: credit ledger, program requirements, degree audit, and graduation decisions.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- New `OpenLearning.Credits` domain reading course completion and certificate outcomes.
- No automatic change to existing course progress records.
