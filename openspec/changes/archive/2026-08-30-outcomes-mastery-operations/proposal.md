# Proposal: Outcomes and Mastery Operations

## Problem

The repository has assessment, gradebook, competency, and analytics specifications, but their operational seam is not defined: instructors need explainable outcome status and intervention signals rather than disconnected scores and charts. Canvas exposes outcome/mastery workflows that are a useful comparison point.

## Change

Connect declared course outcomes to graded activities, calculate auditable mastery states, and surface actionable learner risk signals to authorized instructors and administrators.

## Non-goals

- Automated high-stakes decisions.
- Replacing the existing gradebook or analytics capabilities.
- Hidden or opaque AI scoring.

## Impact

Adds outcome mappings, calculation snapshots, explainable intervention indicators, and role-scoped instructor views.
