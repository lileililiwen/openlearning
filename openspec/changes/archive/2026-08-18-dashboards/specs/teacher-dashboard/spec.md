## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Teacher has a teaching dashboard

The system SHALL provide a dashboard for Instructors showing per-course teaching statistics and quick links into management.

#### Scenario: Teacher views dashboard
- **WHEN** an Instructor opens their dashboard
- **THEN** each of their courses shows enrollment count, paid revenue, completion rate, and quiz pass rate

#### Scenario: Quick actions
- **WHEN** an Instructor views the dashboard
- **THEN** each course links to edit, orders, quizzes, chat, and (where available) roster management
