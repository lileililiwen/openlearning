## Why

Progress and certificates record outcomes but do not support optional points, badges, challenges, or fair leaderboards.

## What Changes

- Add auditable point rules and ledgers, versioned badges, and time-bounded challenges.
- Add opt-in, privacy-aware leaderboards and anti-abuse controls.
- Keep rewards non-monetary and separate from grades/credits.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `gamification`: points, badges, challenges, leaderboards, and abuse controls.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- New `OpenLearning.Gamification` domain consuming trusted completion/event sources.
