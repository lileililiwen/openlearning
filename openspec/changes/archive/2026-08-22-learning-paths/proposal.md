## Why

Course outlines structure one course, but learners and organizations cannot define or follow a sequenced program across courses.

## What Changes

- Add publishable learning paths containing ordered required and elective course groups.
- Track learner path enrollment, prerequisite eligibility, and aggregate completion.
- Give instructors and administrators path-management and learner-progress views.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `learning-paths`: cross-course programs, prerequisites, enrollment, and completion.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- New `OpenLearning.LearningPaths` domain depending on course, enrollment, and progress services.
- New learner and management pages; no course-outline behavior changes.
