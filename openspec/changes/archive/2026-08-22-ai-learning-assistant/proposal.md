## Why

The platform has deterministic assessment scoring but no governed AI assistance for course-grounded Q&A or draft feedback on subjective work.

## What Changes

- Add opt-in, citation-backed course Q&A over instructor-approved materials.
- Add instructor-configured draft feedback and grading suggestions that require human confirmation.
- Add provider abstraction, safety policy, privacy controls, quotas, audit, and cost controls.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `ai-learning-assistant`: grounded learner assistance and human-reviewed grading suggestions.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- New `OpenLearning.AI` domain integrating with course resources, Q&A, assignments, assessments, and moderation.
