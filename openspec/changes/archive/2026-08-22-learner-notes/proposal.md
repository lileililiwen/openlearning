## Why

The incorrect-answer log supports practice, but learners cannot capture private notes tied to courses, lessons, resources, or timestamps.

## What Changes

- Add private rich-text learner notes with contextual anchors and tags.
- Add search, export, and deletion workflows.
- Enforce strict ownership and safe content rendering.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `learner-notes`: private contextual notes, organization, search, and export.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- Extend `OpenLearning.StudyTools` or add a focused notes package following the modular pattern.
