## Why

Courses and assignments do not model placements, workplace supervisors, competency evidence, hour logs, or multi-party practical-training approval.

## What Changes

- Add practical programs, placements, host organizations, supervisors, competencies, hour logs, and evidence.
- Add approval, evaluation, incident, and completion workflows.
- Protect workplace and learner data through placement-scoped access.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `practical-training`: placements, competencies, time/evidence logs, supervision, and completion.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- New `OpenLearning.PracticalTraining` domain; optional integration with organizations, storage, notifications, credits, and certificates.
